Let us understand strategy pattern through a real - world problem scenario

1. The Problem : Shipping Cost Calculation

 Imagine you are building an e-commerce platform. One of the features you need
 is shipping cost calculator. Sounds simple enough, but shipping costs can be 
 calculated in many different ways depending on the business rules : 

    a.Flat Rate: A fixed fee regardless of weight or distance
    b.Weight-Based: Cost incrases with package weight
    c.Distance-Based: Different rates for different delivery zones
    d.Express Delivery: premimum pricing for faster service
    e.Third-Party API: Dynamic rates from carriers like FedEx or DHL
